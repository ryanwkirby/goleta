# The original written rules

The rules of goleta as they were written down, reproduced exactly — including
its spelling and spacing. Nothing here is edited, and nothing should be.

This is the source the rest of the repo is measured against.
[`../HOW-TO-PLAY.md`](../HOW-TO-PLAY.md) says the same thing in plain English,
and [`RULES.md`](RULES.md) says what this app actually does and where the two
part company.

---

> Goleta Rules
>
> 4-8 players.
>
> The goal of the game is to be the last player with cards.
>
> If a card can be played, it must be played.
>
> The gameplay was originally based on crazy 8's and modified over the years for
> timing optimization.
>
> —————————————————————————
>
> A dealer is selected. The dealer will rotate clockwise around the table each
> round of play.
>
> The dealer passes a single card, face-up, clockwise to each player with the
> dealer being the last person to get a card. This is repeated until each player
> has 3 cards. All cards must remain face up and displayed so all players can see
> each other's cards. The remaining deck is placed facedown in the center of the
> players and a single card is drawn from the top of the deck, turned face up,
> and placed next to the remaining deck. This is the seed card.\*
>
> It is now the first players turn. The first player is the player immediately to
> the left of the dealer.
>
> The player has a set of cards in front of them that, depending on seed card,
> can be played on a given turn. A card can be played when either its suit or
> numeric value matches the suit or numeric value of the face up card. The player
> would take the card that can be played from their hand, put it face up on top
> of the face up side of the middle decks, and their turn is over.
>
> The exception to this being an 8 card. 8 cards can be played on top of any card
> with the benefit of the player playing the 8 being able to choose the suit of
> play going forward. The player would place the 8 face up on top of the face up
> side of the middle decks, announce the suit they want to be played, and then
> their turn is over.
>
> If a player has a card that can be played, it must be played. If a player has
> multiple cards that can be played, it is the choice of the player as to which
> card they play.
>
> If a player does not have a card that they can play, they are allowed to draw
> up to three cards from the deck. Cards are drawn one at a time and placed into
> the players hand. When the player draws a card they can play, they must play it
> and their turn is over. If three cards are drawn and no play can be made, the
> players turn is over and the next player is up. If there are no more cards
> remaining in the deck to be drawn from, the face up side of the deck is then
> reshuffled and turned face down. The top card is flipped over and the game
> continues on. If a player was in the middle of drawing cards, they are able to
> continue to draw up to three cards so long as they are still unable to play a
> card in their hand.
>
> If a player has a card they can play but they reach out and touch the face down
> side of the middle decks as if to draw another card, this triggers a Sunny Rule
> (if called out by another player). In a Sunny Rule event, the offending player
> must first play the card they could have played in the regular manner, then,
> from their remaining hand, choose any one of the  cards as a punishment card to
> be played as well. Finally, the card from the face down side of the decks that
> was touched is flipped over to the face up side of the decks and becomes the
> new card in play.  This ends the offending players turn. If nobody calls the
> Sunny Rule out at the time of offense, the game continues as normal for the
> player.
>
> When a player loses all of their hand to the center deck, they have lost the
> game. They exit the game while it continues on without them. This continues
> until only one player remains. They are the winner.
>
> \*If an 8 is drawn as the seed card, it is considered a natural 8, and is played
> like any other card would. The suit shown is the suit played.

## What is not in here

Worth stating, because the repo got it wrong for a while: **the Power of Eights
and Dealer's Choice are not in the original.** Both are alternates this game
added later, and `RULES.md` marked them as the original's until #420. The rule
Dealer's Choice varies — that a seed 8 is a natural 8 — *is* the original's; the
option to override it is not.

Nor is there anything here about a wrong call costing the caller, about naming a
card when you call, or about who deals being anything other than a rotation.
`RULES.md` lists every such difference in one place.
